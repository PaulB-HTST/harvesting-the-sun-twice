What we use

Analytics cookies (only if you accept)

If you press Accept, we load Google Analytics (measurement ID G-SPCV546Z74). It sets cookies named _ga and _ga_SPCV546Z74, which last up to two years and help us count visitors and see which pages are useful. Google receives information about your visit, including your IP address. Until you accept, Google Analytics is not loaded and none of these cookies are set. If you decline, or withdraw consent later, they are not set, and we delete any that were set earlier.

Your cookie choice (stored on your device)

We remember whether you accepted or declined by saving a single value, htst_cookie_consent, in your browser's local storage. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it is not used for tracking.

Page-view counts (only if you accept)

If you press Accept, we also load Vercel Web Analytics, which counts page views without setting cookies and without following you across sites. Like Google Analytics, it is not loaded unless you accept.

Other services this site contacts

To work, pages load some resources from other providers, such as fonts from Google Fonts, code libraries from cdnjs and unpkg, and map tiles and data on the Pipeline Map from OpenStreetMap, CARTO, UK government data services (environment.data.gov.uk) and NASA POWER. Your browser sends your IP address to these providers when it fetches their content. We do not control any cookies they may set under their own policies.

The analysis tool

The Analysis Tool does not save the figures you enter. It returns to its default values each time you visit, and nothing you type is stored in cookies, local storage or the page address.
